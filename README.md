# Hi there, I'm Akib Javed 👋

I am a Spatial Data Scientist, researcher (PhD, LIESMARS, Wuhan University), and the Founder of Maplicon. I specialize in bridging remote sensing, advanced machine learning, and open-source geospatial standards to solve complex environmental and urban resilience challenges. 

Currently, I work as a Consultant on Hydrology and Geoinformatics at C4RE International Ltd. and lead the QGIS Bangladesh User Group.

### 🌍 What I do
* **Spatial Data Science:** Designing automated workflows using Google Earth Engine (GEE), Python, and R for multi-sensor Land Use and Land Cover (LULC) classification and coastal hazard risk assessments.
* **UAV & LiDAR Photogrammetry:** Processing high-resolution spatial data utilizing DJI Matrice 300 RTK and Zenmuse L1.
* **SAR Interferometry:** Exploring Sentinel-1 and NISAR L-band SAR workflows using NASA's ISCE3 for infrastructure subsidence.
* **AI & Machine Learning:** Integrating local open-weight AI models, AlphaEarth Foundations 64-D embeddings, and ensemble models (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) into geospatial analysis.
* **Research:** Published 21 peer-reviewed journal articles focusing on remote sensing and environmental modeling.
