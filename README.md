# Hi there, I'm Akib Javed 👋

I am a geospatial scientist and Consultant on Hydrology and Geoinformatics at C4RE with over ten years of experience in remote sensing and spatial data science. Holding a Ph.D. in Cartography & GIS from LIESMARS, Wuhan University, my academic work includes 42 peer-reviewed publications with over 1,190 citations and the invention of the SISAI and NSAI spectral indices for automatic urban mapping.

Currently, I work as a Consultant on Hydrology and Geoinformatics at C4RE International Ltd. and lead the QGIS Bangladesh User Group.

### 🌍 What I do
* **Consulting & Climate Resilience:** Delivering technical assistance for World Bank and UNDP assignments across the Pacific and South Asia, including the Kiribati Coastal Hazard Risk Assessment.
* **AI Agents & Spatial Data Science:** Highly proficient in using AI agents alongside machine learning and deep learning pipelines (Google Earth Engine, Python, and R) for automated workflows, large-scale spatial modeling, and environmental risk assessment.
* **Academic Supervision & Mentorship:** Supervising academic research and guiding young researchers to conceptualize, design, and structure their studies in remote sensing, GIS, and environmental science.
* **Web Development:** Helping build simple, functional websites with AI.
* **Field Surveying:** Leading national-scale mapping projects and multi-investigator field surveys using KoboToolbox, RTK, GPS, and UAVs.
* **Community Leadership:** Founder Member of the QGIS Bangladesh User Group and Co-founder of the Space & Environment Research Center.
* **Research:** Published 21 peer-reviewed journal articles focusing on remote sensing and environmental modeling.
